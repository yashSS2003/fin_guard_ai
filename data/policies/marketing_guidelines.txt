Marketing Guidelines

Marketing communication should be clear, fair, and not misleading.
Promotional claims must include important limitations, exclusions, and terms.
Comparisons with competitors must be verifiable and supported by evidence.

Avoid aggressive pressure tactics such as "act now or lose everything", "must invest today", or "guaranteed wealth".
Return-related claims must be balanced with risk disclosures.
Testimonials and success stories should not imply that similar outcomes are guaranteed for all customers.

Suggested correction style: replace absolute promises with balanced language, add risk warnings, and include links or references to full terms and conditions.

Digital marketing campaigns must not use countdown timers, artificial scarcity claims, or urgency language that pressures customers into financial decisions without adequate time to review terms.
All fee-related claims including zero-fee, no hidden charges, or unlimited offers must be accompanied by the applicable conditions, duration, and exclusions.
Investment return claims in promotional content must include the relevant time period, benchmark comparison, and a standard risk disclosure.
