General Compliance Policy

All customer-facing and internal financial communications must be accurate, balanced, and auditable.
Statements about financial products must not mislead customers about risk, eligibility, cost, returns, or limitations.
Required disclosures should appear close to the claim they qualify.

Content must avoid guarantees unless the guarantee is contractually documented and legally approved.
Risk warnings are required when discussing investments, lending, credit, insurance, or market-linked products.
Customer data, KYC information, and financial records must be handled according to privacy and consent requirements.

Reviewers should flag missing disclosures, unclear eligibility conditions, exaggerated claims, and statements that may create unsuitable advice.
